A yard that stays soggy, a driveway that washes out, a low spot that never dries — it almost always traces back to grade and drainage that was never dug right in the first place. Jerry Cheshire Land Clearing Services excavates the trenches, pipe, culverts and retention capacity your property actually needs to carry water away instead of holding onto it.

What We Do

Drainage Work That Actually Holds Up

From a single trench to a full retention system, we bring the right equipment and decades of know-how to get water moving the way it should.

Drainage Trenching & Pipe Installation

Trenches dug to grade and pipe installed so water moves off your property, not through it.

Storm Drain & Culvert Systems

Culverts and storm drain lines installed to carry water safely under roads, driveways and low spots.

Retention & Detention Pond Excavation

Retention and detention ponds dug and shaped to hold stormwater and release it slowly and safely.

French Drains & Perimeter Drains

French drains and perimeter drains installed around homes, barns and low areas to carry groundwater away.

Grading for Positive Water Flow

Ground graded to the right slope so water sheds where it should instead of pooling in place.

Erosion & Washout Control

Washed-out and eroded areas regraded and corrected so the problem doesn’t come back.

Questions

Drainage Excavation FAQs

Standing water that doesn’t soak in after a day or two, a soggy yard, a washed-out driveway, or water pooling near a foundation are all signs the grade or drainage isn’t doing its job. We’ll walk your property and tell you straight whether it’s a grading fix, a pipe, or both.

Both. We excavate the trench to grade and set the pipe or culvert ourselves, so the whole system — not just the hole — is built to actually move water.

Every property is different, so we provide a free, no-obligation estimate based on your land, access and the scope of work before anything begins.
