Backfilling

Backfilling Done Right the First Time

Backfilling isn’t just shoveling dirt back in place — done wrong, it leads to settling, drainage problems and foundation issues down the road. Jerry Cheshire Land Clearing Services backfills trenches, foundations and excavations with the right material, placed and compacted in proper lifts.

With over 50 years of experience and equipment built for the job, we backfill everything from utility trenches to full foundation walls for homeowners, builders and contractors across Southeast Georgia.

  • Trenches, foundations & excavations backfilled
  • Proper material placement & compaction in lifts
  • Free, no-obligation estimates
  • Licensed, bonded & insured since 1967

What We Backfill

Backfilling for Every Kind of Project

Foundation Walls

Compaction & Grading
