Demolition & Earthmoving

From Standing Structure To Cleared, Graded Site

Whether it's an old barn, a fire-damaged home or a commercial building that's run its course, Jerry Cheshire Land Clearing Services brings it down safely and hauls off what's left. Structures, concrete and foundations all come out — and the earthmoving to regrade the site afterward is handled by the same crew and equipment, no separate contractor needed.

With the equipment for a small teardown or a full commercial demolition, and over 50 years of local experience, we clear the site down to bare, graded ground and ready it for whatever comes next.

What We Do

Demolition For Every Stage Of The Job

From bringing a structure down to grading the site afterward, we handle every step of the teardown.

Structure Demolition

Homes, barns and commercial buildings brought down safely, from a single structure to a full site.

Concrete & Foundation Removal

Slabs, foundations and driveways broken up and removed, right down to bare ground.

Interior & Selective Demolition

Interior walls, fixtures and partial teardowns handled without disturbing what stays standing.

Debris Hauling & Disposal

Earthmoving & Site Regrading

Final Grading & Site Cleanup

Final grading and cleanup leave the site clear, level and ready for what's next.
