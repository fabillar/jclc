Tree & Stump Removal

Trees And Stumps Cleared, Root To Ready

A tree that needs to come down, or a stump left behind from one that already has, can hold up everything from a new build to just getting your yard back. Jerry Cheshire Land Clearing Services takes down standing and storm-damaged trees and grinds or digs stumps out completely — roots and all — so nothing's left to grow back or get in the way.

With the right equipment for a single stubborn stump or a whole lot full of trees, and over 50 years of local experience, we clear it, haul off the brush and debris, and leave the ground ready for grass, a garden or a build.

  • Tree removal & storm cleanup

  • Root removal & site regrading
