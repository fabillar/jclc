House & Building Pad Construction

A Solid, Level Foundation Starts With the Pad

Every structure is only as good as the ground it sits on. Jerry Cheshire Land Clearing Services builds precisely graded, compacted house and building pads for residential homes, commercial buildings and farm structures across Southeast Georgia.

With over 50 years of experience on local soil and terrain, and a well-maintained fleet that keeps downtime to a minimum, we bring every pad to the right elevation and compact it to a stable base, with drainage sloped away from the structure from day one. No job is too big or too small.

  • Free, no-obligation estimates

How It Works

From First Call to Build-Ready

  1. 1

    Free Estimate

    Tell us about your property and project. We provide a free, no-obligation estimate based on your land, access and the scope of work.

  2. 2

    Plan the Pad

    We talk through your building plans and agree on the size, elevation and scope before any work begins — straightforward, honest pricing with no surprises.

  3. 3

    Grade & Compact the Pad
