Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect information when you visit this website or request a free estimate.

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Jerry Cheshire Land Clearing Services (“we,” “us” or “our”) operates this website located at jerrycheshirelandclearingga.com (the “Site”). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect from visitors and customers, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the Site, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information in two ways: information you provide directly, and information collected automatically.

Information you provide

When you submit our “Free Estimate” contact form, we collect the details you enter, which may include:

  • Your name, email address and phone number
  • The service you’re interested in and your property’s location
  • Your preferred date and best time to be reached
  • Any additional details you choose to share about your project

If you call or email us directly, we’ll also have whatever information you share with us that way.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting and security providers automatically log standard technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, and the date and time of your visit. We use this only for security purposes described below — for example, to prevent automated spam submissions through our contact form.

2. Cookies & Similar Technologies

This Site does not use advertising or analytics cookies. The only cookie-adjacent technology in use is a spam-prevention check (see below) on our contact form that may use limited technical signals to confirm you’re a real visitor rather than automated software. It does not track you across other websites or build an advertising profile.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your free estimate request and follow up about your project
  • Send you a confirmation email acknowledging your request
  • Protect the Site and our contact form from spam and abuse
  • Maintain records of estimate requests and customer communication

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We rely on a small number of trusted service providers to operate the Site and contact form, including:

  • An email delivery service that sends the notifications generated when you submit the contact form (to us and to you as a confirmation).
  • A spam-prevention service that runs a security check on the contact form to block automated bot submissions.
  • A web hosting provider that hosts this website and its contact-form service.

Each of these providers processes information only as needed to perform their function for us, and maintains its own privacy practices. We do not share your information with any other third party except as required by law or to protect our legal rights.

5. Data Retention

We retain contact form submissions and related correspondence for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request, maintain business records, and comply with legal obligations. If you’d like us to delete information you’ve submitted, contact us using the details below and we’ll honor reasonable requests.

6. Your Choices

You’re never required to submit the contact form to reach us — you’re welcome to call or email us directly instead. You may ask us at any time what information we hold about you, request a correction, or request that we delete it, by using the contact details below.

7. Children’s Privacy

This Site is intended for general business audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you share with us, including working with providers that maintain industry-standard security practices. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
