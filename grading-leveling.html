Grading & Leveling

Ground Shaped Right, So Everything Built On It Lasts

A site that isn’t graded right causes problems for years — standing water, washouts, uneven floors. Jerry Cheshire Land Clearing Services grades and levels ground to the right elevation and pitch for homes, commercial sites, yards and driveways across Southeast Georgia.

With over 50 years of experience on local soil and terrain, and a well-maintained fleet that keeps downtime to a minimum, we bring precision grading to projects of every size — new construction, drainage corrections, yard leveling and everything in between. No job is too big or too small.
