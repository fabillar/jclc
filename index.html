About Us

The Excavation Specialists Southeast Georgia Has Trusted Since 1967

Jerry Cheshire Land Clearing Services is a family-owned business built on hard work, honest pricing and equipment that gets the job done right. For over 50 years we've helped homeowners, builders and farmers across Southeast Georgia turn raw, overgrown land into usable ground — whether that's a home site, a private road, a farm pond or a full commercial pad.

We're fully licensed, bonded and insured, and every estimate is free with no obligation. No job is too big or too small.

  • Licensed, bonded & insured since 1967
  • Well-maintained fleet, minimal downtime
  • Residential, commercial & agricultural experience
  • Locally owned and operated out of Waynesville, GA
