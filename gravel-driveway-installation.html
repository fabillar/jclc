Gravel Driveway Installation

A Driveway Built To Last, Not Just Look Good

A gravel driveway is only as good as what's underneath it. Jerry Cheshire Land Clearing Services builds driveways the right way — proper grading, a solid compacted base, and a crown that sheds water — so it holds up to Georgia rain and heavy vehicles instead of rutting out in a year.

Whether you need a brand-new driveway built from the road to your door or an existing one regraded and resurfaced, we handle the grading, drainage, culverts and compaction it takes to get it right, backed by over 50 years of local experience.

  • Graded & compacted base
  • Crown & drainage built in
  • New installs & resurfacing

What We Do

Gravel Driveways Done Right, Start To Finish

From a brand-new driveway to regrading a worn one, we handle every step.

New Gravel Driveway Installation

Full driveways built from bare ground to a finished gravel surface, sized and graded to last.

Grading & Base Preparation

Subgrade graded and a proper base laid down before gravel goes on top.

Drainage & Crown Shaping

Driveways crowned and sloped so water sheds off instead of pooling or washing out.

Culvert & Driveway Entrance Installation

Culvert pipe set at the road or ditch entrance so the driveway ties in cleanly.

Gravel Resurfacing & Regrading

Worn, rutted or pot-holed gravel driveways reshaped and topped with fresh material.

Compaction & Finish Grading

A final compaction pass for a firm, even surface that holds up to traffic and weather.
