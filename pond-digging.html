Pond Digging & Farm Pond Construction

A Pond Built Right Holds Water, Not Problems

A pond that isn’t dug to the right depth, slope and clay base won’t hold water — or won’t hold up over time. Jerry Cheshire Land Clearing Services digs and shapes farm ponds, landscape ponds and retention ponds sized and sloped for your land and your goals across Southeast Georgia.

With over 50 years of experience reading local soil and terrain, and a well-maintained fleet that keeps downtime to a minimum, we bring the right equipment to ponds of every size — from a small farm pond to a multi-acre recreational lake. No job is too big or too small.
