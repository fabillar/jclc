Trenching Services

The Right Trench for Whatever's Going In It

A water line, a sewer line, a run of electrical conduit or an irrigation feed all need their own depth, width and bedding to go in right and stay put. Jerry Cheshire Land Clearing Services digs utility trenches to spec across Southeast Georgia, so whatever you're installing goes in clean and stays protected.

With over 50 years reading local soil, and equipment sized for everything from a short line run to a full site's worth of utilities, we dig to the depth and grade your project calls for — and backfill and compact it properly once the work below is done.

What We Do

Water & Sewer Line Trenching

Electrical & Conduit Trenching

Irrigation Line Trenching

Trenches for irrigation and sprinkler lines dug where they belong, without tearing up the yard.

Septic & Field Line Installation

Precise Depth & Grade Control

Trench Backfill & Compaction

How It Works

From First Call to Backfilled Trench

  1. 1

    Free Estimate

    Tell us what's going in the ground and where. We provide a free, no-obligation estimate based on your land, access and the scope of work.

  2. 2

    Plan the Trench

    We confirm depth, width and route before any digging begins, and coordinate around utility locates as needed.

  3. 3

    Dig & Install

    Our crew and equipment cut the trench to spec, ready for your pipe, conduit or line to go in.

  4. 4

    Backfill & Compact

    Once the line's in, we backfill and compact the trench so the ground stays stable above it.
