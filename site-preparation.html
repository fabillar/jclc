Site Preparation

Build-Ready Ground, Done Right From Day One

Every good build starts with the ground beneath it. Jerry Cheshire Land Clearing Services handles full-scale site preparation — clearing, grading and compaction — for homes, commercial buildings and farm structures across Southeast Georgia.

With over 50 years of experience on local soil and terrain, and a well-maintained fleet that keeps downtime to a minimum, we take your property from raw land to build-ready. No job is too big or too small.

  • Residential, commercial & agricultural sites
  • Free, no-obligation estimates

What’s Included

Everything Your Site Needs Before Construction

From the first tree to the final pass with the compactor, we prepare the ground so your build starts right.

Clearing

Grading & Leveling

Accurate grading and leveling for building sites, yards, driveways and drainage flow.

Compaction

Compacted ground that’s stable and ready to support whatever you’re building on it.
